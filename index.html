HOW TO PLAY

🎮 Controls

  • A / D Move left / right
  • W / Space Jump, or Soda Fart double-jump in the air (🥤)
  • S Belly Slam (land on bosses!)
  • F / Shift Chili Fart Blast (🌶️, recharges)
  • P / Esc Pause
  • R Restart current wave
  • M Mute

💡 Tips

  • Eat every snack to clear the wave. Every 3rd wave is a boss.
  • Chili farts blow up falling veggies and weights for bonus points.
  • Every boss has its own trick, and the intro banner tells you how to beat it. Wave 18 is The Cracker.
  • Slam a boss while it glows gold for double damage. You can stomp minions like Mario.
  • Open up to 5 cases at once. Everything you unbox goes straight to your inventory.
  • Got a pile of junk skins? Trade them up or sell the duplicates.
